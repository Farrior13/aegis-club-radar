B — Behavior Control

Regulating where you live, who you associate with, and how you spend your time and money; requiring permission for major decisions; rigid schedules and sleep deprivation that strip personal autonomy.

I — Information Control

Deliberately withholding or distorting information; banning outside journalism, critical books, or contact with former members; splitting truth into Insider vs. Outsider doctrine; peer surveillance and confession culture.

T — Thought Control

Black-and-white "Us vs. Them" absolutism where the leader/doctrine is always right and you are always at fault; loaded language (thought-terminating clichés); internal thought-stopping techniques to suppress doubt.

E — Emotional Control

Love-bombing followed by conditional worth, guilt, and shame; Phobia Indoctrination (Freedom of Mind, 2012) — implanting irrational terror that leaving the group will cause catastrophe, madness, or total shunning.
