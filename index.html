How Aegis Club Radar Brings Our App & Community Together Through an NPC Club

On-chain bounties are usually solo, fragmented across chains, and hard to discover in time. Aegis Club Radar turns solo bounty hunting into a multiplayer social experience inside our NPC Club:

01

Real-Time Discovery in Club Channels

Our Mini-App streams newly opened bounties from Base, Arbitrum, and Ethereum directly into the Club so members never miss high-value technical, creative, or community quests.

02

Multiplayer Squad Formation

Complex bounties require code, on-chain data verification, and visual proof cards. Club members use the Squad Coordinator to pair developers with designers and share rewards.

03

Peer Review & Proof Verification

Before submitting an on-chain claim, members post their draft deliverable in the Club for rapid peer review—increasing win rates and building reputation across Season One.
